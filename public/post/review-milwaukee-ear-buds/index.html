Review: Milwaukee USB Bluetooth Jobsite Earbuds

These may be the most overpriced PPE I’ve ever bought.

Pros

Both the earbuds themselves as well as their charging case have incredible battery life. The Milwaukee claims that the ear buds will last up to 10 hours and that the charging case will provide the ear buds with up to 70 hours of life. I can definatly vouch that they will out last a 8 hour shift.
